## About Me

I'm a DevOps & Software Engineer with **5+ years of experience** building reliable, automated, and cloud-native systems. My journey started at 13 when I built my own math website — that curiosity grew into robotics, the **MIT MEET program**, a CS degree, and eventually real-world roles in cloud infrastructure and platform engineering.

I've worn many hats: **NOC/SOC Engineer**, **Cloud TechOps Engineer** at [ExLibris](https://exlibrisgroup.com) & [Clarivate](https://clarivate.com), and now full-time **DevOps Engineer** — always with a soft spot for automation, home labs, and building things that actually work.

> *Programming isn't just a profession for me — it's the thing I kept coming back to even when I didn't have to.*
